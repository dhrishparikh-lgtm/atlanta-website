KNOW THE STANDARDS

SERVER RULES

Every member is expected to follow these regulations in our Discord community and in-game roleplay.

SECTION 01

Discord Community Rules

These rules apply to all members across Atlanta Roleplay's Discord server.

01

Nicknames

You are required to have your Roblox username as your Discord nickname. For example: Callsign | Roblox Username. If you are in a department, you must include your department callsign.

02

Drama

Please refrain from initiating or causing drama within Atlanta Roleplay. Keep personal arguments in direct messages and do not bring unnecessary conflict into the community.

03

Respect

All users must treat each other with respect in text channels, voice channels, and everywhere else within our server.

04

NSFW and Inappropriate Content

Do not post NSFW, explicit, or otherwise inappropriate content that may disturb or negatively affect community members. Violations may result in moderation.

05

Safety and Privacy

Do not share personal information belonging to yourself or others, including home addresses, phone numbers, or other private details.

06

Spamming

Do not spam messages, emojis, GIFs, or links. Avoid flooding channels with repeated or off-topic messages.

07

Advertising

Do not advertise other servers within Atlanta Roleplay or send unsolicited advertisements to our members through direct messages.

08

Alternate Accounts

The use of alternate accounts to participate in or gain an unfair advantage within the community is prohibited, especially when used to bypass server moderation.

SECTION 02

Roblox Roleplay Rules

All players must maintain realistic, fair, and respectful roleplay while in-game.

01

Roblox Terms of Service

All players must comply with the Roblox Terms of Use to participate in our roleplay community.

02

Fail Roleplay

All roleplay must maintain a realistic standard. Do not perform actions that would be considered unrealistic or break the roleplay experience.

03

Random Deathmatch (RDM)

Random Deathmatch is prohibited. You may not shoot or kill another player without a valid roleplay reason.

04

Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM)

Vehicle Deathmatch is prohibited. Do not intentionally ram or use a vehicle to attack other players or vehicles without a valid roleplay reason.

05

Fear Roleplay

Fear Roleplay is mandatory in situations involving threats or danger. Players must react realistically when threatened, injured, or outnumbered.

06

New Life Rule (NLR)

If you are killed or jailed, you may not return to the same scene or continue participating in that situation. Treat it as a new life.

07

Leaving to Avoid Punishment (LTAP)

Leaving, resetting, or disconnecting specifically to avoid staff action or active in-game consequences is considered LTAP. If you disconnect by mistake, rejoin and explain the situation to staff.

08

Metagaming

Do not use information your character could not realistically know in-game. Information obtained outside the roleplay must not be used to gain an advantage.

09

Safe Zones

Spawn points are safe zones. Engaging in combat or initiating roleplay conflicts in these locations may result in moderation.

10

Scene Interruption

Do not interrupt ongoing roleplay scenes, including traffic stops, pursuits, and other active situations. Allow scenes to proceed without unnecessary interference.

!

Important Notice

Violations of these regulations may result in moderation or other appropriate staff action. Staff may review situations based on the available evidence and circumstances.

NEED HELP?

Contact Our Staff

If you have a question about the rules or need help with a situation, contact the staff team through our Discord server.
