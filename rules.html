COMMUNITY STANDARDS

Know the rules.

These standards help keep Atlanta Roleplay organised, respectful, and enjoyable. Members are responsible for understanding and following the official rules.

ON THIS PAGE

Community conduct Roleplay standards Session conduct Staff instructions Enforcement
01

Community conduct

  • Treat all members respectfully, regardless of rank or role.
  • Harassment, discrimination, threats, and targeted abuse are not acceptable.
  • Do not spam, flood channels, or intentionally disrupt conversations.
  • Follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  • Do not share private information or recordings without appropriate permission.
02

Roleplay standards

  • Keep actions and decisions realistic for the situation.
  • Do not intentionally ruin scenes or interfere with another player's roleplay.
  • Value fair play and cooperation over winning every situation.
  • Follow the session host's instructions and any announced scenario requirements.
  • Use voice chat appropriately and avoid unnecessary background noise.
03

Session conduct

  • Join sessions prepared to follow the host's briefing and instructions.
  • Do not interrupt briefings or deliberately talk over others.
  • Use the correct in-game roles, teams, and equipment for the scenario.
  • Do not exploit game bugs or use unfair methods to gain an advantage.
  • Follow any additional rules announced for a specific session.
04

Staff instructions

  • Follow reasonable instructions from authorised staff during sessions and in Discord.
  • If you disagree with a moderation decision, raise the issue calmly through the appropriate channel.
  • Do not impersonate staff or falsely claim permissions you do not have.
  • Staff members are also expected to act professionally and treat members fairly.
05

Enforcement and appeals

Rule violations may result in a warning, removal from a session, a restriction, or further moderation action depending on the circumstances and severity.

Do not attempt to evade moderation actions. If you believe a ban was issued incorrectly, use the official ban appeal form.
