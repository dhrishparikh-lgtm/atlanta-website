WHAT WE VALUE

Good staff starts with good people.

These qualities help our team support the community effectively.

01 / ACTIVITY

Reliable participation

Staff should make a reasonable effort to stay involved and communicate when they are unavailable.

02 / FAIRNESS

Professional conduct

Moderation decisions should be handled calmly, consistently, and without favouritism.

03 / TEAMWORK

Working together

Good staff members cooperate with the rest of the team and ask for guidance when needed.
