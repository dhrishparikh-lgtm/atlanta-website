GET TO KNOW US

More than just a roleplay server.

Atlanta Roleplay is a voice-chat-only Emergency Response: Liberty County community built around realistic roleplay, teamwork, and an organised community.

WHO WE ARE

A community built on quality.

Atlanta Roleplay brings players together to take part in organised roleplay scenarios in Emergency Response: Liberty County. We aim to provide an environment where members can enjoy realistic situations, work together, and improve their roleplay skills.

As a voice-chat-only community, communication is an important part of our sessions. Members are expected to respect one another, follow server rules, and contribute to a positive experience for everyone.

01

Realistic roleplay

We encourage sensible decisions, realistic scenarios, and proper roleplay conduct.

02

Clear organisation

Structured leadership and moderation help keep sessions organised.

03

Community first

Respect, teamwork, and fair treatment are expected from every member.

OUR VALUES

What we stand for

The standards that guide our community and our sessions.

01 / REALISM
