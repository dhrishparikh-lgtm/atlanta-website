Our Community

A Place to Roleplay

Atlanta Roleplay | VC Only is a community for players interested in Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC) and organized roleplay.

We aim to provide an environment where members can connect, participate in roleplay, work together, and enjoy their time in the community.

Our community depends on its members, staff, and leadership working together to maintain a welcoming, organized, and respectful server.
