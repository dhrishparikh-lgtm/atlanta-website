COMMUNITY PARTNERSHIPS

Grow together. Partner with ARP.

We are interested in working with organised, active, and professional communities that share our commitment to quality roleplay.

WORK WITH US

Partnerships built on quality.

Atlanta Roleplay aims to build partnerships with communities that are active, well-managed, and relevant to Emergency Response: Liberty County or roleplay.

We value genuine communities and long-term cooperation over member counts alone. Every request is reviewed, and meeting the requirements does not automatically guarantee approval.

Open a support ticket ↗
01

Relevant communities

Partnerships should relate to ER:LC, roleplay, or a relevant community interest.

02

Real engagement

We look for genuine members and meaningful activity, not inflated numbers.

03

Professional management

Partners should have an organised team that can manage its community responsibly.

REQUIREMENTS

Before you apply

Please make sure your community meets the following requirements.

01

Minimum 50 members

Your server must have at least 50 members, excluding bots.

02

Relevant community

Your server must be related to ER:LC or have a clear connection to roleplay.

03

Active and non-toxic

Your community should have real activity and maintain a respectful environment.

04

Organised moderation

Your community must have Discord and in-game moderation teams appropriate to its activities.

05

Follow Discord rules

Your server must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

06

Dedicated affiliate channel

Your server must have a suitable channel for displaying partnership information.

07

No fake engagement

Bots or artificial member activity cannot be used to meet the member requirement.

Important: Partnership requests that do not meet the requirements may be denied. Staff may ask for additional information during the review. Please do not repeatedly open tickets about the same request.
