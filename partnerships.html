REVIEW BEFORE APPLYING

Partnership Requirements

Your community must meet the following requirements before submitting a partnership request.

01

ER:LC Related

Your community must be related to Emergency Response: Liberty County (ER:LC).

02

Active and Non-Toxic

Your community must maintain genuine activity and provide a respectful environment for its members.

03

Organised Community

Your server should have clear organisation, defined responsibilities, and an understandable structure.

04

Follow Discord's Terms of Service

Your community must follow Discord's Terms of Service and applicable community guidelines.

05

Dedicated Affiliate Channel

Your server must have a designated channel for partnership or affiliate announcements.

06

No Bots or Fake Engagement

Member counts and activity must be genuine. Artificially inflated membership or engagement is not accepted.

07

Moderation Teams

Your community must have both a Discord moderation team and an in-game moderation team.

08

At Least 50 Members

Your Discord server must have at least 50 real members. Bots do not count toward this requirement.

!

Read Before Opening a Ticket

Please ensure that your community meets every requirement before contacting our team. Partnership requests that fail to meet the requirements may be denied. We prioritise quality partnerships, genuine activity, and long-term dedication over numbers alone.
